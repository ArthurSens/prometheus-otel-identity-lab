What the experiment supports

Observed transformation rules

01
OTel→Prom

Exporters derive identity

For this fixture, service.namespace + service.name became job; service.instance.id became instance.

02

Receivers reconstruct identity

Prometheus job and instance became the primary OTel resource identity. A round trip was not an inverse mapping.

03

target_info is the bridge

Prometheus-to-OTel consumed it into resource attributes. OTel-to-Prometheus created it to retain resource context.

04

Scrape policy can rewrite the result

An exporter may derive the expected identity, then honor_labels=false replaces it with scrape-target identity and preserves the original as exported_*.
